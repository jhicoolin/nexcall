AI RECEPTIONIST - LIVE CHAT SETUP
Last updated: May 13, 2026

What is included:

- A collapsed live chat tab on the website.
- The tab is quiet by default and does not open like an ad.
- Visitors can choose:
  1. Quick answer
  2. Human follow-up

============================================================
QUICK ANSWER TAB
============================================================

Route:

app/api/chat/nexcall/route.ts

Important:
The launch route uses a tenant-aware front-desk engine with approved NexCall answers and a safety shutoff.

Main files:

lib/live-chat-knowledge.ts
services/receptionist/web-chat-engine.ts
services/receptionist/company-knowledge.ts
services/receptionist/safety-policy.ts

How it works:

The server cleans the visitor question, runs the safety policy, scores it against 2-3 keyword groups, and returns a pre-approved front-desk style answer.

The chat engine currently supports 1M+ possible answer combinations by combining:

- Topic-specific approved answers
- Warm human openers
- Empathy lines
- Business-context bridges
- Operating guidance
- Next-step guidance
- Conversation polish lines
- Practical closing lines
- 2-3 keywords per topic
- Industry-specific business knowledge capsules

This keeps the chat warm without letting it invent policies, pricing, compliance claims, or setup promises.

Every-company support:

The chat does not hardcode a million scripts. It detects the likely business type and uses a business knowledge capsule for that industry. Examples include cleaning, home services, legal intake, healthcare, restaurants, auto repair, retail/ecommerce, real estate, finance/insurance, and general professional services.

Safety shutoff:

If the visitor uses threats, slurs, abusive profanity, sexual content, or unsafe requests, the chat responds politely and marks the conversation as terminated. The frontend then disables the quick-answer input. The human follow-up tab remains available for legitimate business inquiries.

Twilio wording:

The chat should not tell buyers to create Twilio themselves. The buyer-facing offer is done-for-you:

- NexCall manages the Twilio number pool.
- Each client is assigned an AI receptionist number.
- Existing business numbers can forward missed, overflow, after-hours, or all calls into the AI number.
- A client only needs their own Twilio account if they want to own the carrier account directly.
