AI RECEPTIONIST - HUGGING FACE VOICE LAB
Last updated: May 13, 2026

Goal:
Replace robotic browser TTS with warmer, more human demo audio while keeping the website safe to launch.

============================================================
HOW THE SITE USES THIS
============================================================

The website now has three voice layers:

1. Uploaded MP3 demo clips
   Best for launch. Put finished clips in public/audio and set the NEXT_PUBLIC_DEMO_AUDIO_* variables.

2. Optional Hugging Face generation route
   Route: app/api/tts/huggingface/route.ts
   Enable it only after adding HUGGINGFACE_API_TOKEN and setting:

   NEXT_PUBLIC_ENABLE_HF_TTS_DEMOS="true"

   The browser calls /api/tts/huggingface with a scenario ID only. The server chooses the approved script from lib/huggingface-voice-lab.ts and talks to Hugging Face so your token is not exposed.
   Custom visitor text is rejected. Humanized TTS is only for the six scenario demos.

Important:
The live chat does not use TTS. It uses text question-answering plus a human follow-up tab.

3. Browser speech fallback
   Good for testing only. Browser voices are inconsistent and can sound robotic on customer machines.

============================================================
MODEL SHORTLIST
============================================================

Kokoro
Model: hexgrad/Kokoro-82M
Use for: fast, low-cost open-weight voice demo clips.
Why it fits: the model card describes it as an 82M parameter TTS model and notes Apache licensing.
Source: https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M

Chatterbox
Model: ResembleAI/chatterbox
Use for: expressive voice-agent scenarios, multilingual call demos, and warmer receptionist voices.
Why it fits: the model card positions it for voice agents and supports voice prompts and emotion control.
Source: https://huggingface.co/ResembleAI/chatterbox

Qwen3-TTS CustomVoice
Model: Qwen/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-CustomVoice
Use for: custom voice design and more controlled delivery.
Why it fits: useful when the brand needs a private-owned-company voice instead of a generic assistant sound.
Source: https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-CustomVoice

VibeVoice Realtime
Model: microsoft/VibeVoice-Realtime-0.5B
Use for: low-latency streaming experiments and live-call timing tests.
Why it fits: the model card describes realtime streaming text input with low initial speech latency depending on hardware.
Source: https://huggingface.co/microsoft/VibeVoice-Realtime-0.5B

============================================================
RECOMMENDED VOICE PAIRING
============================================================

Appointment Scheduling:
Warm female voice. Calm, front-desk friendly. Prefer Chatterbox or a polished uploaded MP3.

Lead Qualification:
Confident male voice. Consultative, clear, not pushy. Prefer Kokoro or a custom generated clip.

Restaurant Concierge:
Bright female voice. Polished hospitality tone. Prefer Chatterbox.

Customer Support:
Calm male voice. Patient, steady, good for order/support flow. Test VibeVoice Realtime and compare latency.

AI IVR:
Steady male voice. Precise, trustworthy, routing-oriented. Prefer Kokoro for clean short prompts.

Legal Intake:
Empathetic female voice. Slower and reassuring. Prefer Chatterbox or a carefully approved custom clip.

============================================================
LAUNCH CHECKLIST FOR VOICES
============================================================

1. Generate one final MP3 per demo scenario.
2. Listen on laptop speakers, phone speaker, AirPods, and a real phone call if possible.
3. Test accents, interruptions, background noise, and caller corrections.
4. Avoid voices that sound flirty, overexcited, synthetic, or too young for sensitive industries.
5. Put approved clips in public/audio.
6. Set NEXT_PUBLIC_DEMO_AUDIO_* variables in .env.local and Vercel.
7. Keep Hugging Face live generation disabled for paid traffic unless latency and reliability are tested.
